The process

How it works.

Four steps from a first letter to a wardrobe that looks after itself. After the first, almost nothing is asked of you.

I

Apply

A short, private application, about ten minutes of your time. Every application is read and answered personally.

II

The consultation

One private consultation, in person or virtual. Your proportions, palette and the way you live are understood once, properly, and set down in your written Style Direction.

III

Sourcing

Every piece is found for you, across any label, and shortlisted with its price. You approve; fittings, returns and alterations are arranged for you.

IV

Your wardrobe, kept

Month after month, your wardrobe is maintained and refined. Nothing to chase, nothing to remember, and never nothing to wear.

The purpose

The hours once spent shopping are yours again.

No weekends lost to shopping centres. No returns to queue for. No standing before a full wardrobe with nothing to wear on the morning it matters. Getting dressed becomes the easiest decision of your day.

Questions

Before you apply.

Stated plainly, so the commitment is easy to make and easy to leave.

How much of my time does this take?

Very little. One consultation at the start, then a few minutes each month to approve what's been found for you. Everything else is handled.

What happens in a month when I have nothing on?

Your wardrobe plan is still updated on the first Monday and the next season prepared. Quiet months are when a wardrobe is refined rather than added to.

Is the fee separate from what I spend on clothes?

Yes, always. You buy directly from each brand, and no commission is taken from any label, so every recommendation is made for you alone.

Do I have to buy new things?

No. What you already own is used first, and in some months nothing is bought at all. The aim is fewer pieces, held for longer.

What sizes do you work with?

Sizes 4 to 24.

Will anyone know I work with Zarubina?

Only if you choose. Images are requested, never assumed, and a mutual confidentiality agreement is standard for Private Clients.

Minimum term and notice

Three months for The Atelier and The House, six for Private Client. After that, thirty days' written notice ends the engagement.

Payment

Monthly in advance, by automatic card payment on the first of the month. The agreement is signed before the first delivery.

Pausing

One pause in any twelve months, for up to one month, arranged in advance.

Made-to-order pieces

Where nothing on the market answers the brief, a piece can be designed for you. Making it is quoted separately, at a retained-client rate.

Your information

Measurements, palette, preferences and purchase history are held privately and used only for your wardrobe.
